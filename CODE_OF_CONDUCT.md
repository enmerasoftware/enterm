# Code of Conduct

Enterm follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, criticise the work and not the person, and keep harassment of any kind out of every project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (**Security → Report a vulnerability** works for conduct reports too) or contact the maintainers through the organisation profile. Reports are kept confidential.
