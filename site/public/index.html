Why Enterm

A terminal that grew a canvas, not an IDE that hides the terminal.

⌘

Vendor-neutral

Use whichever agent fits the task, or several at once. No account, no subscription, no lock-in.

❯_

Official CLIs, as they ship

Enterm starts the programs you already use. It does not patch, proxy or reimplement them, and never edits ~/.zshrc, ~/.claude or ~/.codex.

∞

Sessions survive quitting

A background service, entermd, owns the terminals. Close the window or update the app; the work goes on and is there when you come back.

▦

A canvas, not a tab strip

Every agent and terminal is a box you can tile, move, resize and group, in project tabs that each remember their own pan and zoom.

!

See who needs you

Permission questions light up the box and collect in one Approvals inbox. Usage limits for Claude Code and Codex sit on the status line.

◌

Light by design

Native AppKit and SwiftUI. Nothing animates or polls while idle, and no telemetry. A feature you do not use costs nothing.

Race

One task, several agents. Keep the best result.

Give the same task to two or three of Claude Code, Codex and Gemini. Each gets its own git worktree from the same commit, so the race is fair and your main folder is untouched.

  1. Live lanes. Elapsed time, files changed, lines added and removed, and a runner heading for the flag.
  2. Medals and tests. Medals mark the finishing order; the results page runs your test command per entrant.
  3. Pick Winner. Merges the winner's branch with --no-ff and removes every worktree and branch the race made. Or drop it all.

      iPhone companion

      Step away. Approve from the lock screen.

      Pair your iPhone with a QR code. When an agent asks for permission, answer from the lock screen with Face ID, follow progress in a Live Activity, and follow and settle races.

      • End-to-end encrypted. Both sides derive a key with X25519 and you compare a six-digit number. The relay only ever sees encrypted bytes, and every connection uses a fresh key.
      • Chat and terminal. Read Claude Code and Codex sessions as a conversation with a reply box, or switch to the terminal.
      • Start sessions and check usage from the phone. You can host your own relay.

      Agents drive the canvas

      A local MCP bridge lets agents open boxes, set up a team, open a git worktree and move board cards. Tools that open boxes need your permission.

      A board for agents

      Move a card to In Progress and it goes to its agent; the agent reports back. Only you can move a card to Done.

      Chat view

      See a Claude Code or Codex session as a conversation, read from its transcript. ⇧⌘M

      Built-in views

      A browser, notes, a whiteboard, files, video, and iOS Simulator and Android emulator boxes beside your terminals.

      Local dictation

      Hold ⌃⌘ and speak. whisper.cpp runs on your Mac, only while you dictate.

      English and Turkish

      The Mac and iPhone apps follow your system language, or the choice in Settings.

      No tokens, no telemetry

      Your subscription tokens stay inside the official CLI. Enterm goes online only for things you can see.

      Signed updates

      Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and updated through Sparkle with an EdDSA key.

      Ports and titles

      The ports a session listens on appear on its box, and untitled boxes show the program's own session title.

      Install

      Install it the way you like.

      Free, no account. macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on Apple silicon.

      Download the DMG

      Open it and drag Enterm into Applications. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized by Apple, and it updates itself.

      Download for Mac v0.1.5

      Or use Homebrew

      $ brew tap enmerasoftware/tap
      $ brew trust enmerasoftware/tap
      $ brew install --cask enterm

      Homebrew asks before it loads casks from a new tap; brew trust answers that once.

      Then install the agents you want the way their vendors describe. Enterm finds them through your login shell, and if one is missing it shows the official install command and runs it in a terminal you can see.

      Questions

      Does it cost anything?

      No. Enterm is free and open source under the MIT licence. There is no account and no subscription. You use the agents with your own plans.

      Will it change my Claude Code or Codex setup?

      No. Enterm never writes to ~/.zshrc, ~/.claude, ~/.codex or any other configuration of yours. Integration arrives through launch arguments and environment variables, and your subscription tokens stay inside the official CLI.

      Which Macs does it run on?

      macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on Apple silicon. Release builds are arm64 only.

      What happens to my sessions when I quit?

      They keep running. A background service, entermd, owns the terminals, and the next launch picks them up.

      Does Enterm collect data?

      No telemetry. It goes online only for things you can see: update checks, the dictation model or an agent's installer when you ask, web pages you open, the Library, and encrypted traffic through the relay once you pair a phone.

      Is Enterm made by Anthropic, OpenAI or Google?

      No. Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and the other CLIs belong to their vendors; Enterm is not affiliated with them. It is made by Enmera.
